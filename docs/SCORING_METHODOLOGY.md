# Scoring Methodology

Source reuse screening is a transparent weighted heuristic:

- Quality 18%
- Condition 18%
- Reusability 20%
- Storage readiness 10%
- Buyer readiness 12%
- Transport fit 10%
- Indicative carbon-saving signal 7%
- Inverse contamination-risk signal 5%
- Inverse processing-burden signal 5%

Candidate matching adds material-grade compatibility, quantity fit and date compatibility to the source screening score. Results are prioritization signals, not certifications.
